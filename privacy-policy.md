# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

Size Guesser ("we", "our", or "the app") is operated by Emirhan Kasal. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information on our own servers. However, the third-party services integrated into the app may collect data as described below.

## Third-Party Services

### Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. If you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads. If you decline, ads will still be shown but will not be personalized. Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### RevenueCat
The app uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect your device identifier and purchase history to validate your subscription status. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### Apple Game Center
The app integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Any data associated with Game Center (such as your Apple ID and game scores) is governed by Apple's privacy policy at: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
- We do not collect location data.
- We do not sell your data to any third party.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
